# learn-quantum-with-phoebe

Quantum for Data Teams: Fine Print First. Sixteen 45-minute sessions on what quantum computing will
and will not do to a data team's workload, taught on a real statevector simulator that runs in every
page (up to ten qubits, 1,024 amplitudes).

- Leader track, six sessions, no code: a qubit is a vector; where the speedup comes from; the
  data-loading fine print; when the classical algorithm catches up; why the data-team playbook
  breaks here; the transfer map from a data team to quantum-ready, including the post-quantum
  inventory.
- Practitioner track, ten sessions, hands on: the simulator from scratch; gates as matrices;
  entanglement measured; Grover step by step; counting queries; the loading-tax calculator; a noisy
  circuit; reading a speedup claim; the post-quantum inventory; the fine-print bench.

Every number on a page was computed first in node from `assets/qs-live.js` and confirmed by the
numpy reference `materials/qs_reference.py` (amplitudes to 1e-9, sampled counts identical). The
engine checks seven properties on every page load. No vendor, machine, roadmap, coin or price is
named anywhere, and nothing here is investment advice.
